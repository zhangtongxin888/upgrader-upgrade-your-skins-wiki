What do 2X, 4X, and 8X mean?+

They are the upgrade targets named by the official listing. The listing does not explain every underlying value or outcome, so this guide does not invent odds or guarantees.

Can upgraded items be traded for real or Roblox items?+

No. The official description says the game uses virtual currency and that all items and upgrades exist only inside this simulator. They cannot be converted into real-world or external Roblox items.

Who made UPGRADER?+

The Roblox experience is by TB1STEP. The official listing currently labels the game BETA 1.3.

How often does UPGRADER update?+

The developer says updates arrive every 3–10 days. Treat that as a stated target, not a guaranteed calendar.
